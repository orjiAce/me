## Finding a charger

BluetanksEV is smart navigation for finding the nearest EV charging station
anywhere in the US, sortable by fastest or cheapest.

## Live location, maps and payments

The live-location architecture: locating stations from the user's current
position, with Google Maps and Google Places API for navigation. The Stripe
payment flow. Expo push notifications. And an admin dashboard for
registered charging stations.

## Stations onboarded

**35,000** charging stations onboarded.
