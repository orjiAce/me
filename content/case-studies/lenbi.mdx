## Context

Lenbi is a peer-to-peer equipment rental marketplace for the Canadian
market: users both rent out and borrow, on phone and tablet. I led the
mobile build in React Native and Expo.

## What I owned

Real-time chat over WebSockets with message history, Firebase Storage
attachments and automatic blocking of personal contact details inside
conversations. Payments run on Stripe — Apple Pay, Google Pay, voucher
checkout, and Stripe Connect for lender payouts with earnings tracking and
bank withdrawal.

Listings sit behind Stripe Identity ID verification, alongside full Google
and Apple sign-in with loading and cancellation states handled. The
multi-step listing flow — photos, pricing, availability, location — is
validated step by step with Formik and Yup. Location search uses
expo-location and Google Places Autocomplete, sorted by distance, filtered
by category, with a date-range availability calendar. OneSignal handles
push, and a rewards-points system covers bookings and referrals.

## Hard parts

Performance on long lists: FlashList and React Query caching. And the iOS
build itself — resolving native conflicts between the Stripe SDK and other
native packages.

## Outcome

The marketplace shipped end to end: chat, payments and payouts, identity
verification, listings, search and rewards, delivered over a ten-month
engagement that ran concurrently with four other contracts.
