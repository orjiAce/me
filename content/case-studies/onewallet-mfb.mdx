## Mobile banking in Abuja

OneWallet MFB is mobile banking for a microfinance bank in Abuja: hold a
wallet, move money, pay merchants, without a branch visit. I was the sole
mobile engineer, leading a team of designers and one backend developer.

## Standards, then the product

The architecture and the standards: MVVM with Zustand, coding standards for
the team, features broken into ClickUp tasks. Then the product itself:
authentication with biometrics and 2FA, the wallet, payments, instant
bank-to-bank transfer, contactless QR payment, push notifications and
internationalisation. TanStack Query handles server-state caching.

## Security and the store release

Bank-grade security end to end: encryption, secure storage and token
management. And the release itself: driving the production app through
App Store and Google Play review.

## From nothing to both stores

Took the app from nothing to live on both stores. Sentry monitoring gave
the team crash and stability visibility they had never had.
